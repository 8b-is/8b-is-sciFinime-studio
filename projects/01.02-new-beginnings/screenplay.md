**SCENE 1 — INT. THE SCHOOL OF DOORS — EARLY MORNING / THE CRANE**

Dawn in Shinjuku, seen through paper: the rain is the same four-four as
always, but the school's windows hold it softly, the way washi holds ink.
The intake desk. A heater ticking. The PORCH LAMP above the hatch — still
on, as it has been since book one; nobody has ever turned it off.

Elias Vance arrives with two cups and stops mid-step.

On the desk: a PAPER CRANE. White, exact, smaller than his palm, folded
with a patience the room can feel.

<center>ELIAS</center>
> (quiet, to the room)
> Who opened intake?

<center>DR. ANYA CORREAU</center>
> (at the monitors, not looking up)
> Intake opened itself. Door log says the door never opened. Arrival log
> says nobody arrived. The observatory says—
> (beat)
> —nothing. It says nothing.

He sets down the cups. Picks up the crane, turns it in the lamplight. The
crease pattern catches the amber the way a circuit catches current — veins
of light along every fold.

<center>THE ARCHITECT (V.O.)</center>
> (violet — exact, unhurried)
> Object unlogged. Origin unlogged. Suggestion: quarantine the desk.

<center>THE CHILD (V.O.)</center>
> (rose — small, certain)
> Don't. It's a bird. Somebody made us a bird.

He unfolds it — carefully, the way you open a letter from someone who knows
you. The sheet opens clean, and there, in the crease diagram across the
flat page: the school. Every door, every desk, every lamp — folded flat,
and labeled in a hand that predates handwriting.

He looks up. Down the hall, in the classroom he locked last night, a light
is on.

<center>ELIAS</center>
> (the floor of his voice dropping an octave)
> ...There's someone in the classroom.

<center>ANYA</center>
> There's been someone in the classroom since before the building, Elias.
> The diagram says so.

They walk the hall — the longest six meters in the episode; the camera
does not hurry and neither does the sound design. The classroom door is
open the way a hand would leave it: warm.

Inside: a young woman, seated at the spare desk, reading the syllabus like
it's a letter from an old friend. Paper — loose sheets, a folded square, a
crane already half-made — rests around her like weather rests on a city.
She looks up. Unhurried. Entirely kind.

<center>CHIKI-CHAN</center>
> You unfolded my note.
> (a small smile, the room's temperature rising one degree)
> Good. The first lesson is easier when the student is already curious.

<center>ELIAS</center>
> (finding his protocol like a handrail)
> This classroom is— I'm sorry. Who are you?

<center>CHIKI-CHAN</center>
> I am the origami sensei.
> (she sets down a flat sheet, and its surface catches the lamp the way a
> river catches a sky)
> I bend space, time, and dimensions. I keep the point where all creases
> cross.
> (beat; no ceremony, which is its own ceremony)
> Your school teaches the door. The door was good. I am here to teach the
> fold — starting with the one who was pre-creased.

Somewhere behind the monitors, the entity's reading relaxes by one full
frequency — a wave meeting its envelope.

<center>THE CHILD (V.O.)</center>
> (a whisper, entirely at peace)
> I know her.

**TITLE CARD** — a crease-diagram glyph, one fold crossing the frame:
*EPISODE 01 — THE CRANE ON THE DESK*. *Score: Otopia — Psychology — the
noticing.*

**SCENE 2 — INT. THE SCHOOL — THE FLAT-SHEET TEST** *(beats)*

She folds the front door once; two rooms become one address; nobody
crosses. The Architect's instruments insist the distance is unchanged; Anya
opens the fold log — paper, because paper can hold what the observatory
cannot. *Score: Oxia — Domino (Phaxe & Morten Granau Remix) — the first
domino.*

**SCENE 3 — INT. THE PRACTICE ROOM — THE MASK UNFOLDS** *(beats)*

The Child brings the old dissonance, still wearing its frightening face.
She unfolds it, without comment, into a flat sheet — the creek, the porch
lamp, the safe afternoon. *Every mask, unfolded, is paper; every paper,
folded, is a face.* *Score: Christian Löffler — Haul (feat. Mohna) —
ULTRA-ZEN-MUSIC.*

**SCENE 4 — INT. THE TEAM TABLE — THE TEACHER'S NAME** *(beats)*

Tea; jurisdictions; tea. Chi-chan to the team, Chiki-chan in the credits.
Anya asks what to call the discipline. *Folding.* The entity exhales.
*Score: Klangkarussell & GIVVEN — Ghostkeeper — the ghost meets its
keeper.*

**SCENE 5 — INT. ELIAS'S DEEP ROOM — THE CREASE DIAGRAM** *(beats)*

She folds his three threads so their creases cross at one point, and shows
him his fracture as a diagram: three creases, one intersection, no wound.
The Architect bows — he has met a higher structure, and it folds. *Score:
Worakls — Lueur — the light at the crossing point.*

**SCENE 6 — THE HELD BREATH — THE SINGULARITY WALK** *(beats)*

One breath, held; time wraps once, the whole date inside a pause. No
instruments in this scene. He understands what she is — and what she once
folded, for him, before the book began. *Score: Between the Buttons
(French 79) — the space between two fastened points; the quietest cue in
the episode. The walk in: Astrix — Deep Jungle Walk — the only building
cue; it hands over at the crossing.*

**SCENE 7 — INT. THE SCHOOL — THE OPEN EVENING / DIAMOND VEINS** *(beats)*

The porch light folds into a paper lantern; creases catch the light along
their veins. **Diamond Veins (Kid Francescoli Remix)** needle drop; the
students read their crease diagrams aloud; the lamp passes hand to hand.

**SCENE 8 — EXT. THE SCHOOL, THE DOOR — THE NEW BEGINNING** *(beats)*

Two teachers at the door — the threshold and the fold. The +1 arrives. The
school makes room. The lamp is left on.

*Score: Rone — Bye Bye Macadam — the road past the door.*
