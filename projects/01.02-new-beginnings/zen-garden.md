## the eight stones of book two

**1 · the crane — chenno's smallest form.** *One wish per fold.* The knock
of book one, answered in paper: a crane on the desk, no door in any log,
a crease diagram of the school inside the wing. The first prayer of book
two is not spoken; it is *left*. *The answered knock.*

**2 · the fold — nearness without crossing.** Two places, one address;
nothing travelled, nothing opened, nothing crossed. The default is
nearness; distance was the anomaly all along. *The law.*

**3 · the mask — unfold gently, never tear.** The mask becomes a sheet;
the sheet was always the creek. Compassion is procedural: at a table,
with tea, without comment. Tearing is the one violence. *The practice.*

**4 · the held breath — the pause that ferries the date.** Time wraps
once; the whole date fits inside the pause before the fold. *Between the
buttons* — the space between two fastened points — is its zazen. *The
ferry.*

**5 · the singular point — where all creases cross.** Not a god to
worship; a place to meet. The point where all your creases cross is not
where you end. *The crossing.*

**6 · the lueur — the light the folded carry.** The veins gather at the
crossing into one patient glow, folded in before the first fold, waiting
behind the paper's skin. Peter remembered Worakls, and the crossing got
its music. *The glow.*

**7 · the lantern — the porch light, folded forward.** The lamp that was
never turned off becomes the lantern that is never put out. The gift made
portable: the school folds its light and hands it on. *The light handed on.*

**8 · the +1 — the school makes room.** The new student arrives; the door
that was built for one becomes a room for whoever comes next. The garden's
oldest gesture, folded again: *sharing is caring.* *The room.*
