## the panel grammar (carried forward + this pass's change)

- **Two geometries, one sheet** continues: the school is hard rectangles, the
  fold is one flowing border. This pass the fold is *social* — it does not
  open a thing, it seats a person. The border that does the seating is the tea
  being poured: one continuous line, warm, unhurried, drawn like the first
  creek-sound entering the art.
- **The creek enters the ear.** Pass 02 held the creek in the art only. This
  pass, the creek *sounds* — rendered once, steady, as the background of every
  panel the teacher is in. It is not a SFX; it is the room's own breath.
- **The lamp bookmark** returns bottom-inner every page, now with company: the
  paper boat (pass 02's grace note) sits beside it from page 3 on.
- **Lettering as material.** Chiki-chan's mincho is now fully on the baseline
  (she is *of* the page). The team's dialogue is the school's sans; Anya's
  professional question is set in a slightly *larger* sans, the one serious
  question the pass allows.
- **Sound.** Two sounds only: the creek (steady) and, once, on page 4, the
  entity's exhale — drawn not as a word but as a single loosening line across
  the whole spread, the wave equivalent of a held breath letting go.
