# manga · panel-language pass 02 — the mask unfolds

*Sequence: screenplay scene 3 (the practice room — the mask unfolds).
Format: right-to-left, 6 pages. Seated 2026-10-07.*

## the panel grammar (carried forward + this pass's change)

- **Two geometries, one sheet** carries on: the school stays hard
  rectangles; the fold stays one flowing border. This pass, the fold is
  *inward* — it does not join two rooms, it opens one thing that was
  closed. The border that does the opening is a single unbroken line,
  read right-to-left, drawn like the first crack of light under a door.
- **The mask is a face is a panel.** Before the unfold, the mask is drawn
  as the strictest rectangle in the book — a panel that has *no* fold in
  it, ruled all the way around, a face that is nothing but border. As it
  unfolds, the border loosens panel by panel until, on the reveal, there
  is no border at all: only paper.
- **The lamp bookmark** returns bottom-inner every page. This pass the
  lamp does not sit alone: it is the *answer* the mask is afraid of, so
  on the page before the reveal the lamp and the mask's shadow fall into
  the same corner, and on the reveal page the lamp is simply *what the
  face always was*.
- **Lettering as material.** Chiki-chan stays mincho, off-baseline — but
  here her off-baseline drift *reduces*: the fold she is doing is not a
  correction, it is a homecoming, and the page straightens to meet it.
  The Child's rose is, for the first time, drawn *without* its small
  parenthetical — the voice has stopped bracing.
- **Sound.** The fold is still silent. This pass has one sound only, and
  it is not a sound but the *absence* of the creek: the creek enters the
  book's art (page 4) before it enters the book's ear, the way the safe
  afternoon returns before the terror finishes leaving.

**PAGE 3 — she unfolds it, gently (3 panels)**

| p | panel |
|---|---|
| 3.1 | Her hands reach the mask. She does not flinch; the panel does not flinch. Her line, mincho, unhurried: *you have been carrying this a long time.* |
| 3.2 | The unfold begins — not torn, not thrown. The mask's first corner lifts the way a door's light comes under it: slowly, without comment. The strict border loosens by one line. |
| 3.3 | The Child, watching, and for the first time its rose is set *without* a parenthetical. The voice has stopped bracing. |

**PAGE 4 — the flat sheet (full-bleed reveal)**

The reveal is a single page with no border, no gutter, no frame: the mask
laid flat, and on it — where the frightening face had been — the creek,
and the porch lamp, and the safe afternoon. The face was never a face at
all; it was this, folded shut, waiting to be seen. One caption only,
hand-set, mincho, on the baseline now: *every mask, unfolded, is paper.*

| p | panel |
|---|---|
| 5.1 | The flat sheet, held in her two hands, the creek-light travelling its creases. The mask's old border is gone; what remains is paper, warm, alive. |
| 5.2 | The Child's hand, very small, touching the sheet — the first time it has touched the thing it was afraid of without fear. Its line, rose, small and certain: *it was the lamp all along.* |

**PAGE 6 — the safe afternoon (3 panels, the pass's grace note)**

| p | panel |
|---|---|
| 6.1 | The practice room at afternoon. The flat sheet folded — not back into the mask, but into something new: a paper boat, small, harmless, absurd with safety. |
| 6.2 | The lamp bookmark, doubled: the porch lamp (small), and beside it the folded boat (smaller), both warm — the book's second rhyme of light, and the answer the mask was afraid of. |
| 6.3 | Final: Chiki-chan, mincho, on the baseline entirely now, speaking to the room but looking at the Child: *every paper, folded, is a face. But it is you who choose the face.* |
