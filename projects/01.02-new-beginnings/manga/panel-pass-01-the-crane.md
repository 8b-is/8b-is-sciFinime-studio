# manga · panel-language pass 01 — the crane on the desk

*Sequence: screenplay scenes 1–2 (the crane → the classroom → the flat-sheet
test). Format: right-to-left, 9 pages. Seated 2026-10-07.*

## the panel grammar (the pass's whole point)

- **Two geometries, one sheet.** The school is hard rectangles — strict
  borders, even gutters, ruled like a building. The fold is a *single*
  flowing border: panels that share one continuous crease line instead of
  separate frames. By page 9 the reader should notice they can no longer
  tell where one room's panel ends and another begins.
- **The fold spread.** Pages 4–5 are one full-bleed spread: the front door
  folded once, the crease running unbroken through the gutter. No panel
  borders on this spread — the page itself is the sheet.
- **The lamp bookmark.** Every page carries one small amber porch-lamp
  panel (bottom-inner corner, 1/6 page), exactly as book one did — but
  here, on the last page, the lamp has company: a second small amber
  circle, the lantern, beside it.
- **Lettering as material.** The school's dialogue is set in the book's
  sans, ruled and even. Chiki-chan's dialogue is set in mincho — calligraphic,
  un-ruled — the only voice in the book allowed a serif. Her lines sit
  slightly *off* the baseline, as if the page had folded under them.
- **Sound.** No SFX for the fold — the fold is silent. Two sounds only:
  the heater's tick (ruler-drawn tick marks) and, entering on page 3, the
  second clean sound — paper, folding — rendered once, steady-handed, the
  way book one drew the creek.

**PAGES 4–5 — the unfold (full-bleed spread)**

The spread is the sheet. Left half: the crane, mid-unfold, wings opening
past the spine. Right half: the flat page — the school's own diagram,
rooms and doors as crease marks, the lamp drawn as the one solid filled
circle. No borders, no gutters. The reader's eye crosses the gutter the
way the fold does. One caption, hand-set off-baseline, the only text on the
spread: *a hand that predates handwriting.*

## continuity notes for pass 02

  is folded — same hand, same patience. Keep the crease diagram consistent
  across passes; the school's map never changes after page 5.
- Chiki-chan's off-baseline dialogue should creep back *onto* the baseline
  by the final pass of the book — the page learning her, as the school
  does.
