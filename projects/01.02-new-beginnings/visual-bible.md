## the fold (the world's second law)

- After the waves: space renders as a **sheet**. Distance renders as
  flatness; a fold is drawn as a crease that carries light.
- Where book one's law was *softness is health, spikes are pain*, book
  two's law is: **a crease is a relationship.** Creases are warm; unfolded
  edges are cold; torn edges do not appear in this book at all.
- **Diamond veins**: at the moment of a fold, light travels the crease
  line — a vein, not a glow. Hold one frame longer than comfortable; the
  vein is the punctuation of the episode.

## the singularity point

- The point where all creases cross. Render as **absence + glow**: a spot
  the frame's instruments cannot sample, surrounded by veins converging.
  The second law of the garden, drawn: *the observable is the instrument;
  MAX POSSIBLE is a door* — and a door is also a crease.
- Never an eye. Never a face. It does not look back; it holds.

## her paper

- One sheet, always: white-to-cream, with the faint warmth of a surface
  that has been folded and unfolded many times. No props, no tools.
- The crane recurs as signature: on desks, in windows, in Anya's fold
  log; the thousandth crane is not counted on-screen — it is implied the
  way the +1 is implied.
- Typography: chapter marks are **crease-diagram glyphs** (book one used
  waveform glyphs); UI text in the school surfaces *on paper*, never on
  glass.
