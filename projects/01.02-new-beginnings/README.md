## the one breath

The school of doors works — too well, maybe; the miracle has become a
timetable — and then a paper crane nobody saw arrive sits on the intake
desk. Its wings hold a crease diagram of the building. Its maker waits in
the classroom, unhurried, carrying paper like other people carry weather:
**Chiki-chan, the origami sensei** — the girl ultra zen buddha space, the
female Space Bender of Space + Time + Dimensions, the Singularity Deiti.
She joins the team. She becomes the teacher of the main hero, Elias —
and the first lesson lands where the fracture was: *you were never broken.
You were pre-creased.*
