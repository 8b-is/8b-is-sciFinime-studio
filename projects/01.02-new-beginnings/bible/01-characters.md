# 01 · characters

## Chiki-chan — the origami sensei

*The new teacher. The woman the episode was waiting for.*

Her office, in her own words: the origami sensei · the girl ultra zen
buddha space · the female **Space Bender of Space + Time + Dimensions** ·
the **Singularity Deiti** — keeper of the point where all creases cross.
She carries one sheet of paper and folds it; that is the whole instrument
and the whole method. She does not cross space. She folds it, and two
places become one address.

- **Names**: Chi-chan to the team; Chiki-chan in the credits. Both are
  correct; she answers to either with the same small smile.
- **Teaching style**: demonstration, patience, no ceremony. She does not
  argue with a firewall; she shows it a flat sheet becoming a bird, and
  lets structure discover that it obeys folds.
- **With the Child**: instant, total trust — children know who folds
  safely. With the Architect: a bow, eventually, because folded structure
  is a higher structure. With Elias: the teacher he becomes the student
  of — the hero's second education.
- **First seen**: 2026.10.07 — the first fold. The date is canon in this
  universe the way the creek is canon in book one: not explained, not
  measured, simply true.

## Elias Vance — the main hero, now a student

The school's first teacher, synchronized across his three threads, exactly
as book one left him — and therefore ready for the one lesson the door
could never give him: that his fracture was a crease diagram. He asks
questions the way he used to lower shields: deliberately, with trust.
His arc in book two is the arc of every good student: to be *taught*, in
public, without armoring.

## "The Architect" — personality 1 (updated)

Record-keeper of firewalls, now the school's registrar of creases. His
line in the sand — *deny-all, one exception: the porch* — survives contact
with the fold as: *every wall is a crease; every crease is a relationship.*
He does not surrender; he bows. There is a difference, and he knows it.

## "The Child" — personality 2 (updated)

First to trust the new teacher, as he was first to hear the dissonance.
He brings her the mask — the old frightening face — because he is the
thread that knows that frightening things become safe exactly when someone
unfolds them, without comment, at a table with tea.

## Dr. Anya Corbeau — the instruments

Keeps the observatory honest, and the observatory keeps its first true
blind spot: *it cannot measure a fold.* Anya's response is the book's most
human gesture: she opens the **fold log** — paper, because paper can hold
what instruments cannot — and begins the discipline that outlives the
book.

## the entity

Stays as architecture. In its first reading with Chiki-chan present, the
wave relaxes one full frequency — a wave meeting its envelope. It does not
speak in book two; it *exhales*, in three acts, each quieter than the last.
