## the one breath

The sword taught mastery. The tea taught presence. The city teaches that
sovereignty is not owning everything — it is owning your own path.

## the seven ages

1. **始まり · Hajimari** — the beginning: Amaterasu, the kami, a country naming itself
2. **平安 · Heian** — the court: the world's first novel, the samurai rising quietly
3. **鎌倉 · Kamakura** — the first shogun: the warrior governs, bushido writes itself
4. **戦国 · Sengoku** — the warring states: Nobunaga, Hideyoshi, Ieyasu, and the tea room
5. **江戸 · Edo** — the peace: the largest city on earth, the floating world blooms
6. **明治 · Meiji** — the opening: Tokyo is named, the sword gives way to the railway
7. **今日 · Kyō** — today, 2026: neon and gardens, and two people finding each other
