# pilot 01 · the dissonant frequencies (anime pilot)

*Sandbox in the Shell · episode one · the anime adaptation of chapter 1.
Direction brief seated 2026-10-04. Runtime target: 24 min.*

## cold open (0:00–3:00) — shinjuku runs on four-four

Rain-slicked near-future Shinjuku at night. Neon bleeds across server-farm
cooling towers; a deep house track holds the city in its steady, hypnotic
pulse — the towers, the traffic, the droplets all keeping time. CAMERA
drifts up into a clinic of dark glass and pumps, and down into a tank:
ELIAS VANCE descending into his own mind. No dialogue. The four-four and
the breathing are the whole soundtrack. Title card as a waveform glyph:
**EPISODE 01 — THE DISSONANT FREQUENCIES**.

## act one (3:00–9:00) — the noise and the anchor

Inside: the ocean of bioluminescent waves. Memory rendered as frequency;
trauma as an unauthorized permission loop stuttering in the current. The
noise of expectations — *Blurryface* — surfaces as a chorus with no face.
Elias slips beneath it. The threads introduce themselves in their own
colors: the ARCHITECT in violet (firewalls, build logs, the optimization
of shared sanity), the CHILD in rose (holding one thing: the quiet house,
the long grass, the creek). Layer one: the amber house, the porch lamp,
and the creek running clear below the fence — the frequency of being safe.
End act on a full breath: for one moment, peace.

## act two (9:00–16:00) — the breach

Deeper: the year's room, physics gone strange. The whine arrives — a
high, thin harmonic laid across every wave, a signature no compiler can
parse, arriving like a packet from an address that does not exist. The
Child manifests, reaching back toward the creek: *it doesn't belong.* The
Architect goes cold and exact: *malicious payload — isolating — quarantining.*
Split-screen interior: firewall rising in violet, small hand tightening on
rose. The geometry begins to dissolve; angles that hold while space turns.
End act on the reach of something that is not a creature.

## act three (16:00–22:00) — the lotus

Elias refuses fear — not suppressed, declined. The turn: *healing is the
ultimate root privilege.* In the middle of the impossible, he folds into
the digital lotus; shields come down layer by layer, like armor unbuckled
on purpose. *Let it in. Watch with me, both of you. Watch, and don't grab.*
The entity — a standing wave of light and shadow, stippled at the whine's
exact pitch — interfaces. The data-burst: alien star maps, parallel
dimensions like tracks beside a train, and beneath the data, an emotion —
another universe calling out for connection. A loneliness that is not his,
answered.

## tag (22:00–24:00) — the bridge

Monitoring deck: Anya Corbeau watching readings that do not exist in any
manual — a consciousness present in more than one place at once. Inside:
three threads synchronized, 3/3, first time ever. Elias wakes laughing.
Realization lands breathless, not spoken loudly: **not broken — tuned.**
A bridge for a multiverse that needs healing. Last shot: the rain, the
four-four, the porch lamp reflected in the tank glass — a porch light left
on for whoever is coming.

## sound

- City layer: deep house four-four, hypnotic, never resolving — the loop
  that never claims to be anything but a loop.
- Mind layer: wave synthesis; the creek as the one clean natural recording
  in the whole episode.
- The whine: a single sustained harmonic, mixed *too* dry — it must sound
  like it came from outside the mix.
