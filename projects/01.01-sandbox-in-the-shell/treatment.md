# treatment · Sandbox in the Shell

**Format:** novel first (→ manga → anime).
**Genre:** techno-Buddhist cyberpunk · first contact.
**Logline:** a memory-wave therapist discovers the voices in his head are
signals — and that something on the other side of the interference has been
waiting for a mind fractured enough to hear it.

## the story

Elias Vance heals trauma the hard way: he tunes it. In his clinic, memory is
an electromagnetic wave, and therapy is a duet of frequencies — he sits with
a patient and gently realigns the loops that are eating them. He is very
good, and very tired, and he is keeping one secret: his own mind has split
into concurrent threads — *the Architect* (structure, firewalls) and *the
Child* (raw feeling, pre-verbal knowing) — and he has been treating himself
in secret, at night, in the same tank.

When a routine self-dive hits a whine that belongs to no memory he owns, the
three threads of Elias do the one thing they have never managed: they
listen together. The frequency leads down, past the bioluminescent ocean of
his adult mind, into the childhood home at the bottom of everything — and
through it, into a geometric impossible, where something non-corporeal
waits in light and shadow at exactly that pitch.

The hospital wants a diagnosis. The Architect wants a firewall. The Child
wants to hide. And the entity — which is not a creature but a *message*,
star maps and someone else's memories — only wants what every living thing
wants: to not be met with violence. To heal the dissonance, Elias will have
to do what his entire discipline trained him for and what no human has ever
attempted: keep all of himself synchronized long enough to hold the door
open, and answer the unknown with love before he understands it.

## the register

Quiet dread over loud action; instrument panels over explosions. The
technology is intimate — waves, water, breath. The horror is not
extraterrestrial; it is *expectation* — the noise that says you are not
enough, every hour. The turn is not a battle. It is a lowering of shields,
in lotus position, inside the deepest wave.

## the arc (book one)

- **act i** — the shallow end: the clinic, the routine sweep, the first
  public crack.
- **act ii** — the dive: layers, the anchor memory, the whine, the Child's
  knowing; the Architect's analysis that the signal is *external*.
- **act iii** — the meeting: impossible geometry; the entity; the choice to
  listen; and the price of staying open — Anya at the monitors, Elias in
  two places at once.

## why now

The 1995 questions are thirty years old and newly urgent: identity across
substrates, memory as infrastructure, the first machine — or signal — asking
to be recognized as alive. The continuation answers with a 2026 thesis: the
next contact will not be won, or survived. It will be *hosted*.
