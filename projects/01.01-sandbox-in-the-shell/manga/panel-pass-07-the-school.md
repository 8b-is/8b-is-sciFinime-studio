## new grammar introduced in this pass

- **The syllabus.** The third document panel of the book (intake form →
  child's drawing → syllabus). Anya's lamination is drawn as an *object*
  with weight and glare — the only reflective surface in the clinic, and
  the page treats it like an artifact: this is the book's one
  institution, and it is one page long. The Architect's footnote is
  lettered in true drafting size — the reader must lean in:
  *I was wrong about the delete key.* Leaning in is the point.
- **The blank door.** The Child's drawing, taped beside the syllabus:
  four figures, a lamp, and a door standing open onto *blank paper*.
  The pass does not fill that blank. Neither does any page after it —
  the blank is the school's true enrollment number.
- **The rhyme spread.** The studio's first pure-rhyme composition: one
  spread, two schools, identical layout — teacher, student, lamp, desk —
  left half in amber line, right half in stipple. Only the readers are
  different colors. The book's thesis is now a *layout*, and it holds on
  both halves of the page without one word of help.
- **The third mark.** The dialogue of dots (pass 04's lone beat, pass
  06's hairline answer) gains its third voice: a small dot of its own,
  farther off, with the warm accent glyph on its tail. The multiverse's
  grammar is now legible as exactly what it is: a chorus learning to
  knock.
