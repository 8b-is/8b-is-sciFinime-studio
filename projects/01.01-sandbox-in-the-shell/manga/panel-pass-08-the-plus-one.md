## new grammar introduced in this pass

- **The round page.** Page 29 abandons panel borders entirely: twelve
  pools of lamp-light define the twelve panels, arranged in the reading
  circle itself — the eye spirals inward the way the evening did. It is
  the only borderless page in the book, and it is the book's thesis about
  institutions: the grid was the city, the circle is the school, and the
  last pages need neither — only lamps.
- **One ink.** Pass 07 let the two registers share a page; pass 08 draws
  every page in the merged ink — amber rules and stipple fused. The
  manga's arc, complete: the city's geometry and the mind's weather
  ended up the same hand.
- **The fifteenth line.** The final document panel of the book: the
  attendance sheet, fourteen names, one blank line — drawn at the exact
  scale and height it would occupy on the wall, which is the exact height
  of the reader's own signature. The book's last invitation, requiring no
  caption and getting none.
- **The last panel.** Book one ends where its first page began — a room
  at night seen from outside — except the lamp is the panel's only light
  and the door, this time, is *open*. One hairline of light crosses the
  empty dark toward the reader's side of the gutter. It is the same mark
  as the smallest knock from pass 06, arriving from the other direction.
