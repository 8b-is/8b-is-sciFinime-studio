# manga · panel-language pass 06 — the other knock

*Sequence: prose chapter 9 (the other knock). Continues pass 05 (20 pages,
55 panels). Format: right-to-left, 4 pages, 12 panels. Seated 2026-10-05.*

## new grammar introduced in this pass

- **Stay, as furniture.** The pass's visual law: *stay* is never drawn as a
  feeling — it is drawn as objects. A second chair pulled up. A lamp left
  on. A stew of small domestic decisions in the middle of cosmology. The
  book has always kept its warm things physical; here the protocol's
  hardest word becomes a *room*.
- **The drafting spread.** The Architect's violet is revealed as
  construction ink: the door arrives as a technical drawing — plan,
  elevation, and a detail callout that says, in drafting lettering, `a
  door is a treaty`. The manga's first full-blown engineering page, ruled
  like the city, warm like the porch. Anya's kill switch is a hand-drawn
  red-line on the sheet.
- **The absence panels.** Two nights pass with the entity gone. They are
  drawn as the only pages in the book *without the standing wave*: the
  governed distance empty, the water still, the bench of light
  unoccupied. No caption explains it. The reader feels the missing
  syllable for two pages, exactly as long as Elias did.
- **The smallest knock.** Pass 04 ended on one ink dot (the fourth beat).
  Here the dot returns — and for the first time in the book, a *second*
  mark answers it: a single hairline across the gutter. The dialogue of
  dots is the multiverse's first sentence, and it takes three days to say
  four characters: `knock · here`.
- **The hand that doesn't, twice.** Pass 05's almost-grab is answered
  here on the other side: the entity, inside its own memory, holds its own
  limb of light back from the small thread. The restraint is mirrored; the
  student is now the one practicing.
