# manga · panel-language pass 01 — the tank scene

*Sequence: screenplay scenes 1–2 (Shinjuku → the descent → the noise).
Format: right-to-left, 5 pages, 16 panels. Seated 2026-10-04.*

## the panel grammar (the pass's whole point)

- **Two geometries.** The city is hard rectangles — strict grid borders,
  even gutters. The mind is flowing panels — borders as waves, gutters
  that merge like currents. The reader should *feel* the grid loosen as
  Elias descends.
- **The porch-lamp bookmark.** Every page carries exactly one small panel
  (bottom-inner corner, 1/6 page) of the amber porch lamp. It never
  advances the plot; it is the book's heartbeat. Page 5 finally lets it
  be the largest light on the page.
- **Lettering as sound.** Blurryface is set as overlapping chorus text —
  same phrase, many weights, crowding the panel edges. The whine (later,
  scene 5) is a single hairline of text, mixed "too dry" — it should look
  like it does not belong to the page's font family.
- **The creek is the only clean sound** — its onomatopoeia is the only
  SFX drawn with a steady hand; everything in the city uses ruler lines.
