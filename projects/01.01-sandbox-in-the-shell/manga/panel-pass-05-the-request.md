## new grammar introduced in this pass

- **The score.** The fourth beat enters the story like a document and
  leaves like music: Hara's annotation lines draw over the water itself,
  marking the knock's rhythm the way a hand marks a score — three strokes,
  a rest, three, and one stroke left alone. The reader learns to read it
  before anyone names it. (The document panel's cousin; the intake form
  taught paper, the score teaches rhythm.)
- **The practiced posture.** The pass's whole turn, in one image: the
  entity, holding still, prints its stipple in the exact silhouette of
  Elias's digital lotus from pass 01 — the guest *sitting* the way the
  teacher sat. No caption frames it as mimicry; the reader is trusted to
  see the student bow.
- **The doorless house.** The entity's home is drawn in the plate's own
  hatch language — and the manga canonizes it: the linocut arch from
  `assets/plates/picks/the-gate.jpg` becomes diegetic, the illustration
  the scene has been quoting all along. The house is vast, lit, beautiful,
  and has no opening anywhere.
- **The child's drawing.** The movement's bookend to the document panel:
  a child's drawing of a door with a lamp over it, on paper, ruled like
  builder's stock. Where the intake form was the institution's warmth,
  this is warmth's blueprint. It is left on the table, the way the Child
  said: big enough for the whole family.
