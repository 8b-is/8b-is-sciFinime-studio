# manga · panel-language pass 04 — the practice

*Sequence: prose chapters 6–7 (the study → the patients). Continues pass
03 (12 pages, 31 panels; episode one closed). Format: right-to-left,
4 pages, 12 panels. Seated 2026-10-05.*

## new grammar introduced in this pass

- **The document panel.** The movement opens with paper as terrain: the
  intake form fills a whole panel, tight-cropped, its ruled lines echoing
  the city's grid from pass 01 — the institution's geometry, repurposed
  for warmth. The first time in the book a page of text is the subject
  rather than the background. The creek question sits inside the ruling
  like water in a gutter.
- **The echo frame.** Sōta's first panel reuses the exact framing of
  Elias's tank-edge panel from pass 01 — same camera, same negative
  space — but the threads are in *his* chest now: violet and rose drawn
  with a shakier hand, thinner. The reader knows before any character
  says it: this is what Elias looked like at nineteen.
- **The fourth beat.** Episode one lettered the knock as rhythm — three,
  pause, three. The movement ends with a single ink dot hanging below
  the gutter line: the first time in the book a sound is drawn as one
  mark. No bubble, no SFX catchline. It is the quietest and loudest dot
  on any page so far.
- **The planed board.** The mind's colors enter the physical world for
  the first time: violet and rose on wood grain. Grief is drawn as a
  board with a bow in it; healing as the same board sitting true, grain
  unbroken.
- **The bookmark, kept.** The porch-lamp bookmark continues its
  every-page tradition; this pass only records its change — the halo.
  The rake is turning.
