# manga · panel-language pass 02 — the breach and the lotus

*Sequence: screenplay scenes 5–8 (the breach → quarantine → lotus →
burst). Continues pass 01 (5 pages, 16 panels). Format: right-to-left,
5 pages, 11 panels. Seated 2026-10-04.*

## new grammar introduced in this pass

- **Late sound.** In the year's room, dialogue and SFX lettering sits
  visibly *offset* from its bubble — text arriving after the mouth. The
  reader should feel the physics lag before any character says so.
- **The whine is not of this font.** A single hairline of text, mixed
  *too dry*: no weight variation, no brush, machine-even. It must look
  like it was set in another book and pasted in. It never gets a bubble.
- **The grid returns as violence.** In pass 01 the Architect's straight
  borders were a refuge. Here the violet lattice overruns the wave panels
  — hard rectangles *inside* flowing borders, multiplying. Safety seen
  from inside begins to look like cage; the art must show both readings
  in one page.
- **The breath strip.** The lotus page carries three tiny panels along
  the bottom margin — in, hold, out — a metronome the reader's eye learns
  to breathe with. They are the smallest panels in the episode and the
  most important.
- **The page break.** The reach (panel 10.1) is the only panel in episode
  one that escapes the page — full bleed to the right edge, border cut
  off. Everything else in the episode is on some kind of leash.
