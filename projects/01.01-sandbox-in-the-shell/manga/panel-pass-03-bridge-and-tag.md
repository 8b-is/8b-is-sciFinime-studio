## the last grammar

- **The square.** The final panel is the only *square* panel in the whole
  episode — hard, still, warm. Every page until now taught the eye that
  squares were the city and waves were the mind; the last panel is a
  square *forgiven*: the grid, finally, holding something soft.
- **The bookmark comes home.** The porch-lamp panel, carried on every page
  since page one, grows to half a page here — the lamp "returns" to the
  real world, reflected in the tank glass, where it started.
- **No words for the realization.** The theme's last beat is lettered with
  nothing at all — one panel, no caption, no SFX. The only silent panel in
  the episode, and the loudest.
