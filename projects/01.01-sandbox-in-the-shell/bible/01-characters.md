# 01 · characters

## Elias Vance

*The primary consciousness.* A brilliant, guarded therapist of **Memory-Wave
Immersion** — the field that translates emotional memory into measurable
electromagnetic frequencies and lets a trained practitioner view and
moderate them inside a subject's mind. He is a techno-Buddhist in practice,
not in costume: the tank is his Bodhi tree, the mind is sacred architecture,
and healing is realignment, never deletion. Elias is exhausted in the way
only the caretakers of others' pain get exhausted — and he has been treating
his own fracture in secret, with the same instrument he uses on patients.

## "The Architect" — personality 1

Logical, precise, obsessed with the structure of the immersion simulation.
Believes the fractured minds are a solvable data problem, and says so, in
Elias's own inner voice, with the calm of a build system. Wants firewalls,
boundaries, deterministic behavior. The Architect is not the enemy of the
book; he is the part of Elias that keeps the lights on while the rest is
lost.

## "The Child" — personality 2

Pure emotion and raw, unprocessed memory. Vulnerable, intuitive, pre-verbal
when frightened. Perceives threats — and *dimensions* — before the others,
the way a child hears the argument behind a closed door. The Child is the
one who first hears the dissonant frequency and knows, without proof, that
it is "wrong, and does not belong".

## Dr. Anya Corbeau

Elias's colleague and only confidant. Initially believes he is suffering
experimental burnout; becomes the key to validating his impossible
observations. She is the book's ground truth — the reader's hand on the
instrument panel. When Elias's vitals spike into patterns no human has
shown, Anya is the first person in the world to see, on equipment, that a
consciousness can be in more than one place at once.

## the entity

Non-corporeal. First appears as a complex, pulsing echo of light and shadow,
fluctuating at exactly the dissonant frequency. It does not speak; it
projects — star maps, memories not Elias's own. It has been waiting for a
consciousness fractured enough to tune in, which reframes the fracture from
diagnosis to invitation.
