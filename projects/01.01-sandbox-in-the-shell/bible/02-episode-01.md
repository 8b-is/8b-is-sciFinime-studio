# 02 · episode 01 — "The Dissonant Frequency"

**Logline:** a sensory immersion therapist discovers that the "memories"
creating his multiple personalities may be signals leaking from parallel
dimensions.

*(the seeded beat sheet — the spine every later pass stands on)*

## 1 · the shallow end

Elias works in his lab — an advanced sensory-deprivation facility. He runs a
routine *memory sweep* on a traumatized patient. This is where the reader
learns **memory as waves**: the technology renders emotional memory as
specific electromagnetic frequencies, and Elias uses smooth, calming
frequencies to soothe the patient's turbulent ones. The work is tender,
exact, and clearly draining him.

## 2 · the fracturing

After the session, the first jarring internal shift. Elias argues with **the
Architect**, who insists on running "maintenance" on their own consciousness
— revealing that they have been treating their *own* dissociative fracture
in secret, with the tank.

## 3 · the dream-in-a-dream simulation

Desperate for stability, Elias enters the tank himself. His own mind is a
vast, shifting ocean of bioluminescent waves. He does not just watch the
memories — he interacts with a simulation of his oldest childhood home
(layer 1). He dives deeper, into a recurring childhood memory (layer 2,
inside layer 1), using a wave-modulating device to navigate: an
Inception-like nested structure where the physics of each layer is its own.

## 4 · the dissonance

In the deepest layer, something goes wrong. A high-pitched harmonic whine —
not a sound but a visual interference pattern across the memory waves.
**The Child** manifests, terrified: the sound is *wrong*, it does not
belong. The Architect analyzes the frequency: it matches **no** known memory
signature of Elias. It is an external wave, interfering with his internal
structure. Their nested dream space is overlapping with somewhere else —
and the signal is leaking in where the psyche is weakest.

## 5 · first contact

Tracing the signal, Elias focuses the waves on its source. The familiar,
structured memory dissolves into an abstract, impossible geometric
landscape. A non-corporeal entity materializes — not a creature: a complex,
pulsing echo of light and shadow, fluctuating at exactly the dissonant
frequency.

## 6 · the cliffhanger

The entity reaches toward Elias in the simulation. Simultaneously, Anya —
monitoring his vitals in the real world — sees his brainwave patterns spike
into levels never recorded, indicating his consciousness is present in
multiple locations at once. In the simulation, Elias asks: **"What are
you?"** The entity does not speak. It projects a torrent of data —
unfamiliar star maps, memories not his own — confirming the entity is
extradimensional, and has been waiting for a consciousness fractured enough
to tune into its frequency.

Elias wakes in the tank, gasping. Inside, the personalities argue — chaotic,
terrified — and for the first time, all of them are listening.
