## the one breath

Chenrezig is the peaceful form; Mahakala is the same compassion, turned
fierce. The Karmapa is the guru who holds the lineage between them. One
compassion, two faces, and the guru who carries it.

## the two texts

1. **The 16th Karmapa Guru Yoga** — *Spontaneously Fulfilling All Desires*: refuge,
   visualization of the Black Crown, the seven-branch prayer, KARMAPA KHYENNO,
   and the absorption of body/speech/mind.
2. **The Chenrezig Sadhana** — *For the Benefit of All Beings*: the HRIH arising,
   the four arms, OM MANI PADME HUM, and the dedication of all beings to the
   same enlightened state.
